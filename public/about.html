About DPG Drip and Gear

Fashion, footwear, watches and accessories — picked with care, delivered nationwide.

Our story

Built for people who care how they show up

DPG Drip and Gear started with a simple idea: good style shouldn't be hard to find, and buying it shouldn't feel like a gamble. Every piece in our catalog — polos, shirts, pullovers, watches, sneakers and more — is picked and checked before it ever reaches you.

We're not a faceless checkout. Every order goes through a real conversation, by WhatsApp or by phone, with someone who can answer your questions before you commit.

What you can expect

  • Every item checked for sizing and condition before it ships
  • Straightforward pricing — offers are genuine markdowns, not inflated "was" prices
  • Delivery anywhere in Nigeria
  • 7-day returns accepted on unsealed items
  • Real replies from a real person, not an automated queue
